Release notes

Two goals drive every release: build the best keyboard lock on the Mac, and lock the keys so you can love the cat. Below is everything that's shipped so far. Cats Lock is built by one developer and one cat, with releases shaped by customer feedback. If something here matters to you (or something's missing), email meow@catslock.app.

v1.6.1

September 7, 2026

Fixes

  • No Dock icon flash. With Hide from Dock on, locking no longer briefly shows a Dock icon.
  • Window stays in front. The app window no longer drops behind others when you toggle Hide from Dock.
  • Smoother widget launch. Tapping the widget no longer flashes the window or a Dock icon.

v1.6.0

August 26, 2026

New

  • One-tap widget. Lock or unlock from your desktop or Notification Center with a single tap, using your current settings. Requires macOS 14 or later.

v1.5.3

August 16, 2026

Improvements

  • Now available on macOS Ventura. Cats Lock now runs on Ventura (13.0) and later, not just Sonoma, so more Macs can join in.

v1.5.2

August 10, 2026

Improvements

  • Fewer false locks. An accidental brush of keys or fast typing is less likely to set off a lock, while a paw on the keys still locks.

Fixes

  • No repeated re-locks. Smart Detect no longer keeps re-triggering after it locks.

v1.5.1

August 1, 2026

Improvements

  • Smart Detect fine-tuning. Fewer false locks during normal use, while a cat on the keyboard is still caught.

v1.5.0

July 20, 2026

New

  • On-screen lock badge. A small badge shows the unlock shortcut and a one-tap Unlock during Stealth Lock and the built-in keyboard lock, so a quiet lock is never a mystery. Thanks to Richard for the request.
  • Lock or free the trackpad. A one-tap button on the badge to lock or free the built-in trackpad.

Improvements

  • Simpler Stealth Lock. The first-run dialog is gone; the badge now shows what's happening.

v1.4.4

July 16, 2026

Improvements

  • Better at catching resting cats. Smart Detect now catches a cat that rests or gently walks on the keys, while leaving your fast typing alone.
  • Clearer "That wasn't my cat" button. Easier to spot and tap on an auto-lock.

v1.4.3

July 10, 2026

New

  • Five sensitivity levels. The Smart Detect slider now has five stops instead of three, including a gentler low setting.
  • Ignore single-key holds. A new option that stops auto-locking when you hold or repeat a single key. Thanks to Saahir for the suggestion.

Improvements

  • Fewer false locks while typing. Smart Detect is better at telling fast typing from a cat. Thanks to Saahir and Juan for the reports.

v1.4.2

July 8, 2026

Fixes

  • App switching stays blocked. Cmd-Tab no longer slips through while locked, even with Hide from Dock on.
  • The main window opens reliably, and in front. It now comes to the front and takes focus every time.

Improvements

  • Sharper cat detection. Smart Detect catches a paw sooner.

v1.4.1

July 1, 2026

New

  • Smart Detect in the menu bar. Reach it in one click from the menu bar dropdown.

Fixes

  • Locking clears Mission Control first. The lock screen no longer gets stranded behind Mission Control.

v1.4.0

June 26, 2026

New

  • Smart Detect (beta). Cats Lock can lock itself when it senses cat-style typing, so you don't reach for the shortcut. Opt-in, off by default, and fully on-device, so your keystrokes never leave your Mac. Thanks to Danielle for the suggestion.

Fixes

  • The lock now covers full-screen apps. The overlay appears correctly over apps in full screen.

v1.3.1

June 22, 2026

New

  • Lock the built-in trackpad too. The built-in keyboard lock can also block the laptop's trackpad, so a cat on the MacBook can't move the cursor while you work on your external.
  • Caps Lock protection. A stray Caps Lock press during a lock no longer leaves you stuck in capitals.

Improvements

  • Hide from Dock is reliable across launch and reopen.

v1.3.0

June 17, 2026

New

  • Lock just the built-in keyboard. Work on an external keyboard? Lock only your MacBook's built-in keys while the external keeps working. Thanks to Steffen for the request.

Improvements

  • New app icon. A cleaner orange cat on a warm sand background, with Dark and Tinted variants.

v1.2.1

June 11, 2026

Improvements

  • Cmd+Tab no longer escapes the lock. The app switcher stays blocked while locked.
  • Always a way out of Stealth Lock. Press Esc while locked to see the unlock shortcut on screen.
  • Smoother, quieter sound. Warning sounds play more reliably and cleanly.

Fixes

  • Hide from Dock works reliably again.
  • Function-key locking prompts for permission correctly.

v1.2.0

June 8, 2026

New

  • Lock the function row (optional). An opt-in toggle blocks the full function row while locked (F1 to F12), including brightness, volume, and media keys.
  • Menu bar icon picker. Choose from three brand-matching menu bar styles. Thanks to Agnes (and Felix) for the request.

Improvements

  • Prevent sleep while locked is now toggleable from the menu bar.

Fixes

  • Caps Lock no longer triggers the warning sound as you lock in.

v1.1.2

June 2, 2026

Improvements

  • Refreshed app icon. A bigger, cleaner cat silhouette, with Dark and Tinted variants.

Fixes

  • Siri voice commands. "Hey Siri, Toggle Cats Lock" (and Enable / Disable) now trigger reliably.

v1.1.1

May 20, 2026

Fixes

  • Correct shortcut label on non-US layouts. Non-US keyboard layouts now show the right key in the shortcut label. Thanks to Agnes (and Felix) for the report.

Improvements

  • Dark and Tinted icon variants for the latest macOS. Thanks to Stephen for flagging this.

v1.1.0

May 19, 2026

New

  • Global lock shortcut. The unlock shortcut now fires from any app. Thanks to Richard for the request.
  • Customizable shortcut. Record your own combo in Settings.
  • Hide from Dock. An optional menu-bar-only mode with no Dock icon. Thanks to Bruce for the request.
  • Prevent sleep while locked. Keeps the display awake so the overlay stays visible if you step away.

Improvements

  • First-run Stealth Lock shows a quick confirmation so you know what to expect.
  • Caps Lock no longer blocks unlock.
  • Launch at Login boots quietly into the menu bar.

v1.0.0

May 15, 2026

New

  • Initial release on the Mac App Store.
  • Lock the keyboard with ⌘L from the window or the menu bar.
  • A friendly overlay with a customizable warning sound on each blocked key.
  • Stealth Lock for silent, invisible locking during meetings.
  • Launch at Login.
  • Fully sandboxed. No account, no analytics.
