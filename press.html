About Cats Lock

Short (50 words)

Cats Lock is a Mac app for people who share a desk with a cat. One click locks the keyboard so your cat can walk across it without derailing your work. Smart Detect locks it automatically when it senses cat-style typing; Stealth Lock stays silent for meetings. $4.99 one-time, sandboxed, no subscriptions.

Medium (150 words)

Cats Lock is a Mac app for people who share a desk with a cat. One click, or a one-tap widget, locks the keyboard from any app, so your cat can nap or walk across the keys without sending chaos into your active document. Smart Detect goes further, locking the keyboard on its own the moment it senses cat-style typing; it runs entirely on your Mac and never reads what you type. A customizable lock screen and warning sound mark each blocked keypress, while Stealth Lock stays silent for meetings, with the option to unlock the trackpad to take control of a call. You can also lock just your MacBook's built-in keyboard while an external stays live. Version 2.0 brings a full redesign, a new mascot, custom sounds, lock profiles, and localization in seven languages. Sandboxed for the Mac App Store, no account, no analytics, $4.99 one-time. Built by indie developer Todd Alexander for him and his cat to enjoy.

Long (300 words)

Cats Lock is a Mac app for people who share a desk with a cat. A customizable shortcut (default ⌘L) locks the keyboard from any app, and you can trigger it just as easily from the main window, the menu bar, or a one-tap widget, so your cat can stretch out or walk across the keys without sending anything into your active document. A lock screen and a warning sound mark each blocked keypress, an audible cue from the next room when your cat has joined the keyboard while you are away.

Stealth Lock swaps that for a silent, invisible variant: the keyboard is still blocked, but no lock screen or sound appears, useful during a meeting, recording, or screen share. You can even unlock just the trackpad, keeping pointer control to take over a call while the keys stay locked.

Smart Detect watches for cat-style typing, such as several keys held at once or rapid scattered bursts, and locks on its own so you never have to reach for the shortcut. It runs entirely on your Mac, reads only the timing and shape of keystrokes rather than anything you type, and offers a one-tap correction whenever it guesses wrong.

For external keyboard users, the built-in keyboard lock freezes your MacBook's own keys while an external keeps working, so a cat can sprawl across the warm keys as you keep typing. The full function row locks too, and you choose whether the built-in trackpad locks with it or stays live.

Make it yours with customizable lock screens, custom sounds, and lock profiles for different situations, all in an interface localized in seven languages. An optional setting keeps the Mac awake while locked so the lock screen stays visible if you step away.

Cats Lock is built fully within the App Store sandbox, with no analytics, no account, and no telemetry. The core lock needs no permissions; only the function-row lock, the built-in keyboard and trackpad lock, and Smart Detect ask for Accessibility, each off until you turn it on. It is a $4.99 one-time purchase, redesigned for version 2.0 and first launched May 15, 2026.
