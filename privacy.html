Cats Lock — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 8, 2026

Cats Lock is a macOS utility that helps you quickly lock your keyboard when a cat (or anything else) is about to walk on it. We respect your privacy and have designed Cats Lock to collect as little data as possible.

What we collect

Nothing. Cats Lock does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no telemetry, no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs, and no tracking of any kind.

Permissions

The basic keyboard lock needs no special permission. A few optional features do, and each stays off until you choose to turn it on:

  • Accessibility. Turning on the function-row lock, the built-in keyboard and trackpad lock, or Smart Detect asks for macOS Accessibility permission once. Cats Lock uses it only to block keys and to notice the timing of keystrokes. It never reads, stores, or sends what you type, and it never uses Input Monitoring.

Smart Detect

Smart Detect is an optional feature that can lock the keyboard on its own when typing looks like a cat rather than a person. It runs entirely on your Mac and reads only the timing and key codes of keystrokes to recognize cat-style patterns — never the characters you type. Nothing is stored, logged, or sent anywhere, and it is off by default.

What's stored on your device

Cats Lock saves your preferences and settings locally on your Mac, inside the app's sandbox — things like your chosen sound, lock modes, Smart Detect sensitivity, lock-screen look, and lock profiles, along with any custom sounds or lock-screen photos you import. The lock's current state is also shared locally with the Cats Lock widget through a private App Group.

This data never leaves your device. Uninstalling Cats Lock removes it.

Microphone, camera, network

Cats Lock does not access your microphone, camera, or network. It operates entirely offline.

Custom sounds and images

When you import a custom sound or a lock-screen photo, the file is copied into Cats Lock's private sandbox container on your Mac. These files are never transmitted to any server.

Apple's App Store

Apple provides standard usage data (downloads, device type, region) to developers through App Store Connect. This is aggregated and anonymous — we cannot identify individual users from it. Cats Lock's rating prompt is Apple's standard system prompt, so we never see who rates the app. See Apple's privacy policy for details.

Children

Cats Lock is rated for all ages. We do not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a future version, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top. Material changes will be announced in the app's release notes.
