Roadmap

Two goals drive every release: build the best keyboard lock on the Mac, and lock the keys so you can love the cat. Below is what's shipped, what's coming, and what's on the radar. Cats Lock is built by one developer and one cat, with releases shaped by customer feedback. If something here matters to you (or something's missing), email meow@catslock.app.

Parking lot features

Add-on features: sharper locking and more delight
  • Maximum Lockdown. An optional extra-strict mode that also blocks system and third-party app shortcuts (Spotlight, app switching, launcher hotkeys), not just typing. Uses the same Accessibility permission as function-key locking, and stays off until you turn it on.
  • Unlock with mouse only. Option to disable the keyboard shortcut entirely and require the on-screen unlock button. For when even a complicated combo isn't safe from a determined paw. Requested by NM (and Socrates).
  • Desktop widget. One-click keyboard lock right from your desktop. No menu bar dropdown, no shortcut needed.
  • Share your cat's masterpiece. Turn whatever your cat typed while the keyboard was locked into a shareable post, laid out on a fun template like a letter or an email. On-device only, and only when you choose to share it.
  • Stats counter. See how many keystrokes Cats Lock has blocked for you.
  • Custom overlay text. Change the message that appears on the lock screen.
  • Voice recording. Record your own short clip to use as a sound. Requested by Agnes.
  • Spa mode. Calming ambient sounds for when the cat wins and you would rather soothe than shoo.

Future: v1.5

SmartLock, in beta
  • SmartLock (beta). Cats Lock starts detecting when a cat is on the keyboard and locks on its own, so you never have to reach for a shortcut. Opt-in, and an early beta to begin with, because getting detection right is hard. This is the purest version of the mission: lock the keys so you can love the cat.

Someday: v2.0

The big one
  • A full redesign. A fresh look for the whole app, plus a new app icon.
  • SmartLock, for real. Automatic cat detection graduates from beta to a polished, dependable feature.
  • Localization. Cats Lock translated into more languages, done once the app and its wording have settled so nothing needs re-translating.
No dates on what's coming. Indie cadence, but the order is roughly the order things will ship.
