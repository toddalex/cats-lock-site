Roadmap

What's shipped, what's coming, and what's on the radar. Cats Lock is built by one developer and one cat, with releases shaped by customer feedback. If something here matters to you (or something's missing), email meow@catslock.app.

Shipped

Available now on the Mac App Store
  • v1.1.2 (May 24, 2026) — Siri voice command compatibility, refreshed app icon with proper Dark and Tinted appearance variants.
  • v1.1.1 (May 20, 2026) — shortcut label fix for non-US keyboard layouts.
  • v1.1.0 (May 19, 2026) — global lock shortcut, customizable shortcut, hide from Dock, prevent sleep while locked, first-time Stealth Lock confirmation.
  • v1.0.0 (May 15, 2026) — initial release on the Mac App Store.
Detailed release notes →

Up next: v1.2

In progress
  • Lock F-row keys. Optional toggle to also block the entire function row including Mission Control, Spotlight, brightness, volume, and keyboard backlight.
  • Menu bar icon picker. Choose from a few icon styles for the menu bar. Requested by Agnes.

Coming after: v1.3

Power-user features behind a new Advanced section
  • Stats counter. See how many keystrokes Cats Lock has blocked for you.
  • Lock built-in keyboard only. Keep external keyboards working while the built-in is locked. Requested by Steffen.
  • Voice recording for the warning sound. Record your own short clip and use it as the warning sound. Requested by Agnes.
  • Custom overlay text. Change the message that appears on the lock screen.
  • More ambient sounds. Purring, spa mode, and other calm sounds for when the cat wins.

Future: v1.4

Lock hardening and quick access
  • Desktop widget. One-click keyboard lock right from your desktop. No menu bar dropdown, no shortcut needed.
  • Unlock with mouse only. Option to disable the keyboard shortcut entirely and require the on-screen unlock button. For when even a complicated combo isn't safe from a determined paw. Requested by NM.

Future: v1.5

Reach more users
  • Localization. Cats Lock translated into more languages, prioritized by where it's finding an audience.

Someday: v2.0

The big one
  • SmartLock. Automatic detection of when your cat is on the keyboard. We have ideas. We'll figure it out eventually.
No dates on what's coming. Indie cadence, but the order is roughly the order things will ship.
